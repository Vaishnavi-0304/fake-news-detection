TF-IDF Vectorization

Converts unigrams and bigrams into statistical frequency weights, capturing vocabulary nuances and context signals across journalistic texts.

Logistic Regression

A high-precision, interpretable linear classifier from Scikit-Learn that evaluates probabilities and separates authentic news from deceptive claims.
